We're hiring on behalf of a Haystack partner!


The Role

• Design and build autonomous agent systems to solve complex, multi-step problems with minimal human intervention.

• Define the architecture and evaluation frameworks for agentic workflows, ensuring safe, predictable, and scalable operation in production.

• Design, develop, and maintain efficient, reusable, and reliable code.

• Implement complex software solutions that meet business requirements.

• Collaborate with cross-functional teams to define, design, and ship new features.

• Identify and resolve performance bottlenecks and bugs.


What You'll Need

• 5+ years of professional software development experience.

• 5+ years of programming experience in at least one software programming language.

• 5+ years of experience leading design or architecture of new and existing systems.

• Experience as a mentor, tech lead, or leading an engineering team.

• Experience with the full software development lifecycle, including coding standards, code reviews, source control, build processes, testing, and operations.

• Familiarity with generative AI tools and a willingness to learn effective prompting and evaluation practices.


What's On Offer

• Competitive annual salary ($168,100 - $227,400 USD) plus potential for sign-on payments and restricted stock units.

• Comprehensive benefits package including health insurance, 401(k) matching, paid time off, and parental leave.

• Opportunity to work on innovative Gen AI solutions for product development workflows.

• A collaborative and inclusive engineering culture.
